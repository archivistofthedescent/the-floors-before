# Generating character art

Character art lives at `assets/art/<character-slug>.jpg` and appears at the top of every part for that character.

- The Mordecai and Carl images were generated by Claude in the cloud thread with Google's Gemini image model (Nano Banana), at 1024x1024. Prompt pattern: "Cinematic gritty TV-season poster style, painterly photoreal, fire-lit and moody. <scene from the character's backstory, with its recurring object>. High detail, no text, no logos."
- To be filled in by Claude on the Pi: the method used to make `assets/art/season-one-cover.jpg`, so nightly drafts can make matching art.
